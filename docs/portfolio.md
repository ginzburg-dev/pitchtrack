# Portfolio presentation

The demo should open with a 10–15 second annotated sequence, then show image-space
tracking beside the pitch view and the per-frame calibration status. Include one failure
case where pitch coordinates become `null` while player IDs remain available.

The public SoccerTrack v2 presentation clip is a **virtual PTZ crop derived from a fixed
panoramic camera**. Put that disclosure on the video and in its caption. It demonstrates
the software contract and controlled camera motion; it is not evidence that physical PTZ
calibration has been solved.

The interview evidence should be the separate SoccerNet comparison:

1. official DeepLab baseline;
2. TVCalib adapter;
3. PitchTrack hybrid temporal calibration;
4. valid/predicted/invalid coverage, official JaC@5/completeness, recovery, jitter, and
   runtime latency on the same split.

Do not publish SoccerNet source clips or model weights unless their terms explicitly
permit it. Do not show a score that has not been generated by its stated evaluator.
